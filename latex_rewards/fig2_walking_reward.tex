\subsection*{Figure 2: Walking Imitation ($1.25\;\mathrm{m/s}$, \texttt{StableTrackingEnv})}

At each $50\;\mathrm{Hz}$ control step, the reward combines an alive bonus, exponential joint/root/end-effector tracking kernels, an action-rate smoothness penalty, and (during Mode~2 Phase~1 soft curriculum) a quadratic normalized MTG torque-excess penalty:
\begin{equation}
r_t^{\mathrm{walk}} =
\begin{cases}
0, & \text{if } z_t < 0.45\;\mathrm{m} \;\lor\; |\theta_{p,t} - \theta_{p,t}^{\mathrm{ref}}| > 0.70\;\mathrm{rad}, \\[6pt]
0.10 + 0.30\,r_q + 0.25\,r_{\mathrm{rot}} + 0.15\,r_{\mathrm{vel}} + 0.10\,r_x + 0.10\,r_z + 0.10\,r_{\mathrm{cart}} + p_{\mathrm{act}} + p_{\mathrm{MTG}}, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where the individual terms are defined as:
\begin{align}
r_q &= \exp\!\Bigl(-30\,\frac{1}{7}\lVert \mathbf{q}_{j,t} - \mathbf{q}_{j,t}^{\mathrm{ref}} \rVert_2^2\Bigr), \\
r_{\mathrm{rot}} &= \exp\!\Bigl(-25\,(\theta_{p,t} - \theta_{p,t}^{\mathrm{ref}})^2 - 1.0\,(\dot{\theta}_{p,t} - \dot{\theta}_{p,t}^{\mathrm{ref}})^2\Bigr), \\
r_{\mathrm{vel}} &= \exp\!\Bigl(-10\,(v_{x,t} - v_{x,t}^{\mathrm{ref}})^2\Bigr), \qquad
r_x = \exp\!\Bigl(-5\,(x_t - x_t^{\mathrm{ref}})^2\Bigr), \qquad
r_z = \exp\!\Bigl(-20\,(z_t - z_t^{\mathrm{ref}})^2\Bigr), \\
r_{\mathrm{cart}} &= \exp\!\Bigl(-15\,\frac{1}{6}\lVert \mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{ee},t}^{\mathrm{rel}} - \mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{ee},t}^{\mathrm{rel,ref}} \rVert_2^2\Bigr), \\
p_{\mathrm{act}} &= -0.01\,\lVert \mathbf{a}_t - \mathbf{a}_{t-1} \rVert_2^2, \\
p_{\mathrm{MTG}} &=
\begin{cases}
-0.50\,\lVert \bar{\mathbf{e}}_{\mathrm{MTG},t} \rVert_2^2, & \text{Mode 2, Phase 1 (\texttt{soft})}, \\
0, & \text{Mode 1 or Mode 2, Phase 2 (\texttt{hard})}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}
Here $\mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{ee},t}^{\mathrm{rel}} \in \mathbb{R}^6$ stacks the pelvis-relative $(x, z)$ positions of the left sole, right sole, and trunk tip, and $\bar{\mathbf{e}}_{\mathrm{MTG},t} \in \mathbb{R}^7$ is the sub-step-averaged normalized biological torque excess over the dynamic MTG envelope.
